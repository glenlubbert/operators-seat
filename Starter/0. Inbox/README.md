# Inbox

Drop anything here: documents, notes, a spreadsheet, a project's old files, a screenshot. Then tell your AI:

> Sort my inbox.

It reads each file, tells you in a line what it is, and proposes where it goes: `1. Projects` (has a finish line), `2. Areas` (ongoing, like a client, your health, your team), `3. Resources` (reference), or `4. Archive` (done, keep for the record). It also pulls out anything worth remembering and shows you before saving. Nothing moves until you say yes.

Some AI apps can write files but not move them. If yours can't, it gives you a short numbered list ("drag this file into that folder"), you do the dragging in Finder or File Explorer, and it checks that everything landed where it should.

Start small. One project's files, or the ten documents you'd hand a new assistant on their first day. Not ten years of Dropbox.

Private things (health, money, client records) stay in their own folder. The AI will ask before reading anything that looks like one of those.
