# 2. Areas

*The parts of your work and life you keep up with indefinitely. No finish line.*

Examples at work: your brand and voice, the people you work with, your goals, a team, a key client. In life: health, home, family, money, a practice you keep up.

A good test: if you'll still be looking after it a year from now and there's no "done," it's an area.

## Adding an area

Say:

> I want to add an area for [my health / the house / my team].

The AI asks what keeping it up well looks like and anything it should know, then creates a folder here with a short note. If it's private (health records, money, client notes), say so; the AI marks the folder private, and nothing in it leaves the folder or goes into your main memory without your yes.

## Included to start

The first interview suggests a starting set of areas (for a business: Sales, Marketing, Finance, Operations, Product development; for a life: Health, Career, Money, Family, Relationships, Friends, Personal growth, Spirituality, Fun, Home) and makes the ones you pick. These three are always here:

- `people/`: one note per person who matters.
- `brand/voice.md`: how you sound in writing.
- `goals/`: your goals for the quarter, and a monthly check on how you're doing.

Add or drop areas to match your life. Each one makes the AI more useful without you having to explain it again.
