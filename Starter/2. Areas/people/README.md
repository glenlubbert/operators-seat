# People

*One note per person who matters. The AI reads it whenever that name comes up, and before drafting anything to them.*

## What to say

> Add [name] to my people.

It asks who they are, how you know them, and what matters for working with them, then creates the note. Later:

> I talked to [name] today. [What happened.]

It adds the conversation to their note and updates when you were last in touch. You never edit these by hand.

## Why notes and not a contact app

A contact app is good at phone numbers and bad at the story: what someone cares about, what you promised them, the thing they hate being called. A note holds all of that. Keep your contact app or CRM for the data; use this for the story.

## What not to put here

These are notes about someone else. Keep to what helps you work with them or stay close to them. Leave out their health, their money, and their private life, even if they told you.

## Staying in touch

The weekly stale check (step 7) lists anyone you haven't been in touch with in a month. Pick two or three to reach out to. When you do, tell the AI, and the list takes care of itself.
