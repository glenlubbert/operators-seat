# 4. Archive

*Inactive items from the other three buckets. The graveyard you don't want to throw away.*

What goes here: past Projects (the whole folder, intact), Areas you no longer maintain, Resources you stopped pulling from.

## Why not delete

Past projects are the best context for new ones: last year's campaign is the closest analog to this quarter's. And Archive is the relief valve that keeps the other buckets clean; if you're not sure something is still active, moving it here is the safe move.

## What the agent does here

Nothing, by default. The agent doesn't read Archive unless you say "check Archive for past campaigns that targeted physicians." Then it goes looking.

## Naming

Prefix with the end date so it sorts chronologically: `2026-03 - [Original Name]/`.
