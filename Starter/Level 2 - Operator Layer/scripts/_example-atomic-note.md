# Weekly review moves from Friday to Thursday afternoon

The weekly review now happens Thursday at 3 p.m., because Friday afternoons kept getting taken by client calls.

## What was decided

From the week of September 21, the weekly review (the Friday audit in the runbook) runs Thursday at 3 p.m. for 20 minutes. The stale check (feed 1) moves to Thursday at 2 p.m. so its list is ready. The calendar event was moved the same day.

## Why

Four of the last six Friday reviews were skipped or cut short because clients book Friday afternoons for end-of-week calls. Thursday at 3 has been free in every week since July. Doing the review a day early also means anything it surfaces can still be handled before the weekend.

## What would change it

If Thursday reviews get skipped twice in a month, or if client calls move to Thursdays, revisit the slot. Check again at the end of the quarter regardless.
