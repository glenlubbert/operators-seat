# Build Log

*The design record for your operating system: what you added or changed, why, and whether it's worth sharing. One entry per change, newest first. A year of entries is the history of how you learned to work with AI, and the raw material for anything you write about it later.*

*The agent appends an entry whenever it adds a file, a rule, a feed, or a connector. You edit the "why" if the agent got it wrong.*

**Shareable?** Yes means a generic version is worth passing to someone building their own. No means it's specific to your stack.
