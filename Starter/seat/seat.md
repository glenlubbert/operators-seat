## The questions this seat runs on

*These come from the Solution-Focused approach to coaching, which starts from what the person wants and what's already working rather than from what's wrong. The AI uses them in the first-run interview, the Friday audit, and whenever you're stuck. Ask the AI to reword them in your voice.*

- **Best hopes:** What are your best hopes for this week (this project, this conversation)?
- **Already working:** What's going well that should keep going? When did this last work, even a little, and what was different?
- **Suppose:** Suppose next Friday went just the way you'd want. What would you notice first? What would the people around you notice?
- **Scale:** On a scale of 0 to 10, where are you on this right now? What's keeping it from being one lower? What would one point higher look like?
- **Smallest next step:** What's the smallest thing that would tell you this is moving?

The AI asks; you answer. It doesn't answer these for you.
