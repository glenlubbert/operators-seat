Operator's Seat

An AI chief of staff for you and your business, built for privacy and portability.

Download Operator's Seat (free)

A ZIP of plain folders and a one-page guide. Works with the Claude desktop app, ChatGPT's desktop app, or an AI that runs on your own computer. Version 0.9.

Why I built it

For too long, I had to keep re-explaining myself and constantly uploading files in each AI conversation or repeat myself when switching between AI apps. Sometimes it made it into memory, or I built a skill around it. Then I'd switch to a different AI and start from scratch. So I began keeping it all in plain text files, in the same folders I've used since 2017 for my life and my businesses.

Those folders now run my company: marketing, finance, operations, and product development. I don't re-explain anything, and it isn't tied to one AI. People kept asking how, so I packaged it. Whether you run a business or the business of your life, it's your own chief of staff, in files you own.

What makes it different

Your files, not their app. Everything your chief of staff knows is kept in folders on your computer, where you can see it and change it, not inside an AI company's memory.

Switch AIs anytime, or use two. Use Claude on Monday and ChatGPT on Wednesday on the same folders, or have one double-check the other's work. No second setup, nothing lost.

Private when you need it. Point it at an AI running on your own computer and, set up fully locally, nothing leaves your machine.

Uses less of your AI plan. It reads a one-page map of what you know, then opens only the notes it needs, so a bigger archive barely costs more per question. And because everything is saved to files, you can start a fresh chat anytime without re-explaining yourself or re-uploading files.

You think. It types. It interviews you, files what you decide, and brings things back to you every Friday. The decisions stay yours.

Seven steps, about a week

  1. Copy the folders and let it interview you.About half an hour. You never open a file.
  2. Catch it being wrong.Correct it once, and it saves the correction.
  3. Tell it five true things,or drop in a few files and let it sort them.
  4. Fill in one project.It knows the deadline from then on.
  5. Give it one rule.The folder starts getting smarter, not just bigger.
  6. Do the Friday audit together.Fifteen minutes, every week. This is the habit.
  7. Run the first feed.It checks what's gone stale, so Fridays start with a list.

Before you start

This is version 0.9. It's built from a system I use every day, and it's now being tried by its first outside users. The download includes a short pilot checklist. If you try it, I'd love to hear where you got stuck. You'll need a desktop or laptop (the phone apps can't open folders on your computer) and a paid AI plan that lets the AI work in a folder: Claude Pro or higher, or ChatGPT Plus or higher, as of October 2026. No paid plan? It can also run on a free, open-source AI on your own computer, which takes more setup.
