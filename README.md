# Operator's Seat

**An AI chief of staff for you and your business, built for privacy and portability.**

**[Download the latest version (ZIP)](https://github.com/glenlubbert/operators-seat/releases/latest/download/Operators-Seat.zip)** · [Project page](https://glenlubbert.github.io/operators-seat/)

Operator's Seat is a set of plain folders and a seven-step guide that turns the AI you already use into a chief of staff that knows your work. It interviews you, keeps what you decide in files on your own computer, and works with the Claude desktop app, ChatGPT's desktop app, or an AI running on your own machine. Switch AIs anytime; the folders come with you.

No coding, no GitHub account needed. Download the ZIP, open `START HERE.md`, and follow the steps. You'll need a desktop or laptop and an AI plan that lets the AI work in a folder on your computer: as of October 2026, Claude Pro or higher, or ChatGPT Plus or higher. A free open-source AI on your own computer also works, with more setup.

## What's in the download

- `START HERE.md`: the seven steps, in plain language.
- `Cheat Sheet - Operator's Seat.pdf`: the seven steps on one page.
- `Starter/`: the folders you copy. They become your system.
- `Why it works.md`: the reasoning, for when you're curious.
- `Pilot checklist.md`: for early users, to note what happened at each step.
- `REUSE.md`: what you may do with it.

## Status

Version 0.9. Built from a system the author uses daily, now being tried by its first outside users. The procedures have been checked in simulation; live use is what the pilot is for. See the testing notes in `Why it works.md`.
